# A selection of developed projects

## OrbiTox (2025)

Visualization platform for NAMs and read-across exploration of multi-domain data.

[https://orbitox.org/](https://orbitox.org/)

## BioBricks.ai (2025)

Versioned data registry for life sciences data assets. Developer and scientific advisor.

[https://biobricks.ai/](https://biobricks.ai/)

## Integrated Chemical Environment (ICE)

Scientific lead while supporting NICEATM. Web resource for chemical data and in vitro / in silico tools.

[https://ice.ntp.niehs.nih.gov/](https://ice.ntp.niehs.nih.gov/)

## DASS App (2024)

Computational application of internationally harmonized defined approaches to skin sensitization.

[https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-023-05617-1](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12859-023-05617-1)

## ChemMaps v2 (2023)

Webserver to navigate and mine an extended environmental chemical universe.

[https://apps.sciome.com/chemmaps/](https://apps.sciome.com/chemmaps/)

## PFAS-Exchange

Collaborated on enhancing the PFAS-Exchange platform (Ruby).

[https://pfas-exchange.org](https://pfas-exchange.org)

## Tox21BodyMap (2020)

Webtool allowing a user to identify target organs in the human body where a substance is estimated to be more likely to produce effects.

[https://apps.sciome.com/bodymap/](https://apps.sciome.com/bodymap/)

## InterPred (2020)

Platform to predict interference chemicals based on large-scale chemical screening.

[https://apps.sciome.com/interferences/](https://apps.sciome.com/interferences/)

## Key characteristics of carcinogens

Literature and assay mapping for the key characteristics of carcinogens.

[https://keycharacteristics.org/](https://keycharacteristics.org/) · [SysRev project](https://www.sysrev.com/p/3588)

## RealityConvert (2017)

Software tool and associated website to convert molecular objects to high-quality 3D models directly compatible for AR and VR applications.

[http://www.realityconvert.com](http://www.realityconvert.com)

## Neighborhood (2017)

Data-mining tools to analyze the neighborhood of selected ionizable groups.

[https://github.com/ABorrel/ionic](https://github.com/ABorrel/ionic)

## LSRs (2016)

Method to extract local structural replacements from the Protein Data Bank, optimized for phosphate groups.

[https://github.com/ABorrel/LSRs](https://github.com/ABorrel/LSRs)

## PockDrug-Server (2015)

Druggability model to predict if a protein pocket can bind a drug-like molecule. The web server also includes tools for pocket analysis.

[PockDrug-Server](https://pockdrug.rpbs.univ-paris-diderot.fr/cgi-bin/index.py?page=home)

## GOHTAM-server (2012–2015)

Detection of horizontal transfers based on a combination of parametric methods. (no longer available)
